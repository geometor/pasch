About¶

Extracting Historical Semantic Knowledge & Repurposing It for Exact Symbolic Reasoning

About This Edition¶

The GEOMETOR edition of Moritz Pasch presents the foundational treatise that established modern mathematical axiomatics and resolved the hidden diagrammatic assumptions of Greek and projective geometry:

  • Moritz Pasch (Vorlesungen über neuere Geometrie, 1882): Author of the 1882 Giessen lectures that supplied the missing topological foundations of classical deduction. By isolating the nuclear axioms of linear betweenness, formulating Pasch’s Planar Separation Axiom (IV. Grundsatz), and reconstructing projective ideal elements strictly from finite sheaves (Bündel) without appeal to infinity, Pasch formulated the modern hypothetico-deductive criterion directly inherited by David Hilbert, Bertrand Russell, Oswald Veblen, Fr. Petrus Hoenen, and Alfred Tarski.

This critical edition extracts the implicit deductive architecture of Pasch’s treatise into an addressable, machine-readable semantic corpus. Every proposition, definition, betweenness relation, and planar transversal step is indexed with stable anchors, linked to synchronized four-track codices (English translation, original German print witness, conceptual summary, and scholarly notes), and verified symbolically in geometor.pasch.order.

The GEOMETOR Mission¶

GEOMETOR is an ongoing research initiative founded by phi ARCHITECT dedicated to recovering, formalizing, and revitalizing foundational geometric and philosophical heritage through three pillars:

  1. Extracting Historical Semantic Knowledge: Moving beyond passive scans to recover the living deductive structure of geometric treatises—elevating verbatim prose, primary-source folios (via publish folio), and calibrated Ink-to-Vector .euclid-graphic diagrams into structured semantic graphs.

  2. Exact Symbolic Grounding (``geometor.model``): Anchoring geometric constructions in 100% exact symbolic algebra (SymPy) and Rational Trigonometry invariants over exact algebraic fields ($\mathbb{Q}$, $\mathbb{Q}(\phi)$). In geometor.pasch, linear betweenness and triangle transversals are verified with exact orientation determinants and signed inner products, demonstrating that planar separation holds free from floating-point distortion.

  3. Repurposing Knowledge Across Mediums: Re-deploying extracted semantic structures across synchronized multi-track Book Codices and EPUB 3 editions (photon-platform/publish), computable deductive canons (geometor.elements, geometor.staudt, geometor.hilbert), interactive SVG laboratories, and visual broadcasts.

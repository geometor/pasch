===========================================
Bilingual Mathematical & Axiomatic Glossary
===========================================

.. rubric:: Technical German & English Terminology of Moritz Pasch (1882)

.. _term-zwischen:

**Zwischenbeziehung (Zwischen)** — *Linear Betweenness*
   :person:`Pasch <pasch>`'s primitive ternary order relation "$C$ lies between $A$ and $B$ on the straight segment $AB$" (:cite:`1882 § 1, Grundsätze I–VIII <pasch:1882>`), axiomatizing open segments prior to congruence or measure.

.. _term-pasch-grundsatz:

**IV. Grundsatz der Ebene (Axiom von Pasch)** — *Pasch's Planar Separation Axiom*
   :person:`Pasch <pasch>`'s planar order axiom (:cite:`1882 § 2, p. 21 <pasch:1882>`): if a coplanar straight line meets one side $AB$ of a triangle $\triangle ABC$ internally and passes through no vertex, it must also meet internally one (and by *10. Lehrsatz*, p. 25, **only one**) of the other two sides $AC$ or $BC$.

.. _term-stammbegriffe:

**Stammbegriffe (Kernbegriffe)** — *Primitive / Nuclear Concepts*
   In :person:`Pasch <pasch>`'s axiomatic methodology (1882 § 12), the irreducible undefined primitives (point, straight segment, planar surface, congruence) out of which every derived geometric concept must be explicitly defined.

.. _term-stammsaetze:

**Stammsätze (Grundsätze / Kernsätze)** — *Nuclear Propositions (Axioms)*
   In :person:`Pasch <pasch>`'s 1882 § 12 manifesto (p. 98), the complete system of foundational axioms distilled initially from empirical observation, after which every theorem must follow by pure logical deduction without any appeal to spatial intuition or diagrams.

.. _term-getrennte-paare:

**Getrennte Punktepaare** — *Mutually Separating Point Pairs*
   Two pairs $(A, B)$ and $(C, D)$ on a closed projective line such that each pair divides the carrier into two segments containing one point of the other pair; satisfied by every :term:`harmonic tetrad <harmonisches-gebilde>`.

.. _term-unendlich-fernes-element:

**Unendlich fernes Element (Scheinpunkt / Scheingerade)** — *Ideal Element (Point / Line at Infinity)*
   A direction or parallel sheaf adjoined to ordinary space so that every two coplanar lines meet in a unique point and every two planes meet in a unique line; reconstructed strictly from finite proper segments via sheaves (*Bündel*) in :cite:`Pasch 1882 §§ 8–9 <pasch:1882>`.

.. _term-reciprocitaet:

**Gesetz der Reciprocität** — *Law of Reciprocity (Projective Duality)*
   The structural symmetry of three-dimensional projective space whereby points and planes are dual primitive elements while straight lines are self-dual, and in a single plane points and lines exchange incidence roles.

.. _term-vollstaendiges-viereck:

**Vollständiges ebenes Viereck** — *Complete Plane Quadrangle*
   Four coplanar points (no three collinear) joined pairwise by six sides (three pairs of opposite sides) intersecting in three diagonal points; plane-dual to the complete quadrilateral (*vollständiges Vierseit*).

.. _term-harmonisches-gebilde:

**Harmonisches Gebilde (Harmonische Punkte)** — *Harmonic Form (Harmonic Tetrad)*
   Four collinear points $A, C, B, D$ such that two opposite sides of a :term:`complete quadrangle <vollstaendiges-viereck>` pass through $A$, two opposite sides pass through $B$, and the two remaining diagonals pass respectively through $C$ and $D$. Satisfies $(A, B; C, D) = -1$ without any appeal to distance.

.. _term-geometrie-der-lage:

**Geometrie der Lage** — *Geometry of Position (Pure Projective Geometry)*
   The pure synthetic science of spatial incidence, order, harmonic separation, and polarity, developed without any reliance on metrical magnitude (length, angle, area, or numerical ratio).

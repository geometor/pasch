Pasch
=====

**Moritz Pasch** (*1843–1930*)
   Professor of Mathematics at the University of Giessen. In *Vorlesungen über neuere Geometrie* (:cite:`1882 <pasch:1882>`), he established the first rigorous axiomatization of linear :term:`betweenness <zwischen>` and planar triangle separation (:term:`Pasch's Axiom <pasch-grundsatz>`), constructed projective ideal elements strictly from finite sheaves (*Bündel*) without appeal to infinity, and articulated the modern hypothetico-deductive criterion of formal deduction (§ 12, p. 98).

Klein
=====

**Felix Klein** (*1849–1925*)
   Author of the Erlangen Program and memoirs on non-Euclidean geometry (:cite:`1871 <klein:1871>`, :cite:`1873 <klein:1873>`), who identified the continuity gap in von Staudt's Fundamental Theorem that directly motivated Pasch's order axiomatics.

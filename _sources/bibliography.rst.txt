Bibliography

.. rubric:: Primary Editions, Axiomatic Foundations & Historical Critical Studies

Primary Source Archived in ``resources/vorlesungen_1882/sources/``
------------------------------------------------------------------

.. _cite-pasch-1882:

* **Pasch, Moritz (1882).** *Vorlesungen über neuere Geometrie*. Leipzig: Verlag von B. G. Teubner, 1882 (iv + 202 pp.). First axiomatization of linear betweenness (§ 1), planar triangle separation (*IV. Grundsatz*, § 2, p. 21; *10. Lehrsatz*, p. 25), ideal sheaves (§§ 8–11), and the hypothetico-deductive method (§ 12, p. 98). Public-domain German witness archived in ``resources/vorlesungen_1882/sources/pasch_1882_vorlesungen_de.txt``.

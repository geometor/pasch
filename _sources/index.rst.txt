============
Moritz Pasch
============

The mission of this project is to capture a semantic digital library of the works of :person:`Moritz Pasch <pasch>` (1843–1930), the German geometer whose 1882 *Vorlesungen* established the axioms of linear order, planar separation, and modern hypothetico-deductive rigor.
